The first chapter

A library, taking shape.

This is a working design preview with example books, inspired by Carol’s Library. Explore the spines, filter by genre, and open a book.

The proposed structure is simple: your finished books up top, the books you want to read below, and a quick search to add the next one.

Once the layout is settled, the next stage is live title / author / ISBN search, permanent storage, ratings, and personal notes.

No account or data storage is connected yet. Changes reset on refresh.
